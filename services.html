Our Services

Comprehensive fertilizing and lawn nutrition solutions for every yard, every season.

What We Offer

Every service is tailored to your lawn's specific needs — no one-size-fits-all treatments.

🌿

Lawn Fertilizing

Our core service. We apply the right nutrients at the right time to promote thick, green, healthy grass all season long.

  • Granular & liquid applications
  • Slow-release nitrogen formulas
  • Balanced N-P-K programs
  • Safe for kids & pets
🧪

Soil Testing & Analysis

Before we treat anything, we get the facts. Soil testing reveals exactly what your lawn is missing so we can fix it right.

  • pH level testing
  • Macro & micro-nutrient analysis
  • Written results & recommendations
  • Custom correction plan included
🗓️

Seasonal Programs

Our most popular option. A multi-visit program that covers your lawn from spring green-up all the way through fall winterizing.

  • 4–6 application rounds
  • Pre-emergent weed control
  • Summer stress protection
  • Fall winterizer treatment
🌾

Lime & pH Correction

Acidic soil blocks nutrient uptake no matter how much fertilizer you apply. We correct pH so everything works the way it should.

  • Pelletized lime application
  • Sulfur treatments for alkaline soil
  • Post-treatment follow-up test
💧

Liquid Aeration & Soil Conditioner

Compacted soil? Liquid aeration loosens the ground to let water, air, and nutrients reach the root zone where they're needed.

  • No machine ruts or mess
  • Covers the entire lawn evenly
  • Pairs well with fertilizing
🍂

Fall Winterizer

The most important application of the year. A late-season treatment that stores energy in the roots so your lawn bounces back strong in spring.

  • High-potassium formulas
  • Root strengthening treatment
  • Cold tolerance improvement
