Our Story

Dr. Lawn was born from a simple belief — every homeowner deserves a lawn they're proud of, without being overcharged or oversold on things they don't need.

What started as a one-truck operation serving neighbors in the community has grown into a trusted name in local lawn care. Over the years, we've built our reputation one satisfied customer at a time.

We're not a franchise. We're not a call center. When you contact Dr. Lawn, you're talking to the person who will actually show up at your door.
