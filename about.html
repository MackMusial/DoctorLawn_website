A family-owned business built on hard work, honest service, and a genuine love of great-looking grass.

Our Story

DoctorLawn was born from a simple belief — every homeowner deserves a lawn they're proud of, without being overcharged or oversold on things they don't need.

What started as a one-truck operation serving neighbors in Southeast Michigan has grown into a trusted name in local lawn care. We've built our reputation one satisfied customer at a time across Wayne, Monroe, and Oakland counties.

We're not a franchise. We're not a call center. When you contact DoctorLawn, you're talking to the person who will actually show up at your door.
